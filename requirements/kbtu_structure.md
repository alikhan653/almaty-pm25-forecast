# KBTU Master's Thesis — Structure Requirements

Source: pre-defense presentation template observed from KBTU 7M06106 cohort
(Makulbay A., 2026). Confirmed against standard Kazakhstani master's thesis
requirements (SТ РК 1.5-2004 technical writing standard).

---

## Mandatory sections (in order)

### Front matter
1. **Title page** (Appendix A template) — university, school, thesis title in CAPS,
   author full name, program code 7M06106, supervisor + consultant names with degrees,
   normcontrol name, city, year. Page counted but number NOT printed.
2. **Abstract (Аннотация)** — **3 languages, 1 page each, in this order: English → Kazakh → Russian**.
   Each page must contain: relevance, aim, object, methods, results/novelty/significance,
   thesis volume/structure, figure/table counts, source count, keywords (5–7), publications.
   Page counted but number NOT printed.
3. **Table of contents** — all headings to 3rd level with page numbers. NOT numbered.
4. **Normative references** — list of standards referenced.
5. **Definitions, designations, and abbreviations** — if used.
6. **Introduction** — starts on new page.

### Body (chapters)
The body must cover all of the following elements (mapped to our chapter structure):

| Required element | Our chapter |
|---|---|
| Relevance of the research | Introduction §Motivation |
| Research goals and objectives | Introduction §Aim and objectives |
| Research methods | Chapter 2 (Methodology) |
| Theoretical and practical significance | Introduction §Practical significance |
| Theoretical and practical novelty (≥ 2 points) | Introduction §Scientific novelty |
| Overview of current research (literature review) | Chapter 1 |
| Results of the research | Chapter 3 |
| Conclusion | Conclusion |
| Publications by the master's student (if any) | Appendix or separate section |

### Back matter
- **References** — APA 7th edition, minimum 30 sources, ≥ 50% from last 10 years.
- **Appendices** — labelled A, B, C; include source code excerpts, extended tables,
  screenshots.

## Chapter length targets

| Chapter | Target (pages) |
|---|---|
| Introduction | 3–5 |
| Chapter 1 — Analytical review | 15–20 |
| Chapter 2 — Methodology | 10–15 |
| Chapter 3 — Implementation & Results | 15–20 |
| Chapter 4 — Discussion | 5–10 |
| Conclusion | 2–3 |
| **Total body** | **60–80** |

---

## Section heading conventions

- Level 1: `1. CHAPTER TITLE` — bold, centred, all caps, new page.
- Level 2: `1.1. Section title` — bold, left-aligned, sentence case.
- Level 3: `1.1.1. Subsection title` — bold italic, left-aligned.
- No level beyond 3 in body text.
- Each chapter starts on a new page.

---

## Scientific novelty — mandatory phrasing

Must appear verbatim in the Introduction. Minimum 2 novelty points. Each point
must be a complete sentence stating what is new and why it is different from
existing work. Example pattern:

> "For the first time, a PM2.5 forecasting model has been calibrated specifically
> for the low-cost sensor network and inversion-dominated winter meteorology of
> Almaty, Kazakhstan, using an open and fully reproducible pipeline."

---

## Publication requirement (MANDATORY)

At least **one** of the following before defense:
- 1 article in WoS/Scopus-indexed journal (Q1–Q4 quartile) matching thesis topic, OR
- 1 article in KOKНВО-recommended journal + 1 conference presentation

Co-authorship with 2–3 others (including supervisor) is allowed.
Conference can be online or offline.

- [ ] Draft of all 4 chapters (may have TODOs, but structure complete)
- [ ] At least 25 references in `thesis/references.bib`
- [ ] At least 5 figures (system architecture, results plots, app screenshots)
- [ ] Abstract written in 3 languages (EN + KZ + RU)
- [ ] Presentation slides (10–15 slides, 10 min oral)
- [ ] At least 1 publication or conference submission confirmed
