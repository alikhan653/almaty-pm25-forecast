# Formatting Requirements — GOST 7.32-2017 + KBTU

KBTU master's theses follow GOST 7.32-2017 ("Report on research work").
Deviations specific to KBTU are noted where known.

---

## Page layout

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Paper size | A4 (210 × 297 mm) |
| Left margin | 30 mm (binding side) |
| Right margin | 10 mm ← OFFICIAL (was incorrectly listed as 15 mm) |
| Top margin | 20 mm |
| Bottom margin | 20 mm |
| Page orientation | Portrait throughout (landscape allowed for wide tables/figures) |

## Typography

| Element | Font | Size | Style | Spacing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Body text | Times New Roman | 14 pt | Regular | **Single (одинарный)** ← official; NOT 1.5 |
| Chapter heading (L1) | Times New Roman | 16 pt | Bold | Before: 12 pt, After: 6 pt |
| Section heading (L2) | Times New Roman | 14 pt | Bold | Before: 10 pt, After: 4 pt |
| Subsection heading (L3) | Times New Roman | 14 pt | Bold Italic | Before: 8 pt, After: 2 pt |
| Figure/table caption | Times New Roman | 12 pt | Regular | Single |
| Code blocks | Courier New | 11 pt | Regular | Single |
| Footnotes | Times New Roman | 12 pt | Regular | Single |

First line indent: 1.25 cm (paragraph indent). Do NOT use blank lines between
paragraphs — use paragraph spacing instead.

## Page numbering

- Arabic numerals, **bottom centre, font 12 pt** (smaller than body 14 pt).
- Title page counts as page 1 — number NOT printed.
- Abstract and Table of Contents also counted — numbers NOT printed on them.
- Continuous numbering from title page through appendices.
- References and appendices continue the same numbering.

## Tables

- Caption ABOVE the table, **left-aligned, no indent**, format:
  `Table 1.1 – Title of the table` (number, em-dash, title on one line).
- Allowed numbering: continuous across whole thesis OR within section (e.g. 3.1, 3.2).
- Every table must be referenced before it appears: "…as shown in Table 3.1."
- Column headers: sentence case (capital first letter only), no period at end.
- Smaller font inside table is allowed (e.g. 12 pt).
- Two blank lines above and below each table.
- Continued tables: write `Continuation of table 1.1` above the continuation.
- Wide tables: move to Appendix rather than forcing landscape in body.

## Figures

- Caption BELOW the figure, **centred, no indent**, 14 pt:
  `Figure 1 Title of the figure` (no dash, no period at end).
- Numbering: continuous Arabic numerals across whole thesis.
- Every figure must be referenced before it appears: "…in accordance with Figure 2."
- Minimum resolution: 300 DPI for raster; vector preferred.
- Colourblind-safe palettes required (Okabe-Ito or Paul Tol).
- Figure must be readable when printed in greyscale.
- **Self-contained captions** — reader must understand without body text.

## Equations

- Display equations: centred, numbered at the right margin in parentheses:
  `(3.1)`, `(3.2)`.
- Inline equations: use proper math notation, do not use `*` for multiplication.
- Every symbol introduced in an equation must be defined immediately after.

## Code listings

- Use a distinct code block style (monospace font, slightly indented).
- Caption: `Listing N — Description` above the block.
- Line numbers optional but recommended for listings > 10 lines.
- Do NOT include large code dumps in the body — put them in Appendix.
  Body listings ≤ 20 lines.

## Lists

- Use numbered lists for sequential steps or ranked items.
- Use bullet lists for unordered sets.
- No more than 2 levels of nesting.
- Each list item ends with a semicolon (;) except the last, which ends with a
  full stop (.). This follows GOST list punctuation convention.

## Abbreviations

- Define every abbreviation at first use: "boundary layer height (BLH)".
- After definition, use the abbreviation consistently — do not alternate.
- Compile a List of Abbreviations if ≥ 5 abbreviations used.

## References in text

- APA in-text: (Author, Year) or Author (Year).
- Multiple citations in one parenthesis, sorted by year: (Chen, 2006; Zhang, 2010).
- Ibid / op. cit. not used — always use full APA citation.
